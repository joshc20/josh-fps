Rivals performance tips

1
Keep your phone cool

Heat can make a phone reduce performance. Take a break if it gets hot.

2
Use a steady connection

Choose a reliable Wi-Fi or mobile connection while playing.

3
Adjust in-game graphics

If gameplay stutters, try lowering Roblox graphics and see whether it feels smoother.

4
Close apps you don't need

Reduce background activity before starting a game.
